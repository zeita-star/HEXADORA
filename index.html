    How Hexadora works

    1. Observe. Note exactly what the computer does: lights, sounds, messages.
    2. Find a guide. Search, browse, or take the quiz.
    3. Test one thing at a time. Follow the steps and tick them off.
    4. Verify, or ask for help. Confirm the fix, or stop and consult your teacher.

    Educational troubleshooting means finding likely causes by testing safely, not guessing. A cause listed here is a possibility until your own checks confirm it.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does Hexadora scan my computer?

    No. It never inspects your device or collects data. Guides and the quiz only use what you tell them.

    Will these steps fix every problem?

    No. Devices differ, so treat each guide as a learning path, not a guaranteed fix.

    When should I stop?

    Stop if a step involves opening electrical equipment, risks your files, or feels beyond your skill. Ask your teacher or a qualified technician.

    Is my progress saved?

    Progress, favorites and theme are saved only in your own browser, when it allows.

    About Hexadora

    Hexadora is a free learning resource for Grade 12 TVL-ICT students. It is a static website with no accounts, no tracking and no data uploads.

    It is not an official product of Microsoft or any hardware maker, and it is not a replacement for your teacher or a qualified technician.
